# Lessons

- Prove the requested core fantasy before polishing a genre shell. For a Minecraft-like game, terrain generation, exploration, mining, placing, inventory, crafting, persistence, and shared block edits must be convincing before any combat work.
- Treat “shooting alike” as an optional interaction style, not permission to replace the requested block-world product with an arena FPS.
- Do not publish or register a game merely because its infrastructure works. Product-loop acceptance comes before distribution.
- When adding competitive shooting, preserve the approved procedural Minecraft world exactly; reduce its playable boundary instead of replacing its terrain with a purpose-built arena.
